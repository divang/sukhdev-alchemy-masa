Returns and Refunds Policy

Last updated: 08 June 2026

1. Return Eligibility

  • Returns are accepted only for damaged, tampered, or incorrect items.
  • Issue must be reported within 48 hours of delivery with unboxing photos or video.

2. Non-returnable Items

Opened consumable products are non-returnable unless quality issues are confirmed.

3. Refund Process

  • After verification, refunds are initiated to the original payment source.
  • Bank or gateway settlement timelines may vary by provider.

4. Contact for Return Support

Email care@sukhdevialchemy.com with order ID, issue details, and proof.
